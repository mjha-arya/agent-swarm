## Reading taken

A new three-layer architecture could easily become a second, competing source of rules. It must sit *under* the Constitution, not beside it.

Adopted chain: **session > Constitution > `CONTRACT.md` > soul > mind > body.** A soul may tighten a Constitution article, never loosen one.

## Decision

**ADOPTED.** `CONTRACT.md` header, `README.md` non-negotiables, every soul template.

## Wrong if

- The chain creates deadlock: two documents both claim a case and neither yields. Then precedence must be resolved by an explicit tie-break rule, not more prose.
- Or: the swarm needs a rule the Constitution forbids, and the temptation is to route around it. That is the signal the rule is wrong — change it in one place, deliberately.

## Eval

Not measurable directly. Proxy: any incident traced to "the swarm did X because its own docs said so, against the Constitution" is a failure of this refinement.
